About & Vision

The Bristol Centre for Artificial Intelligence (AI) and Earth Observation (EO) brings together machine learning and satellite remote sensing to understand how our planet is changing. We are a multidisciplinary group spanning Geographical Sciences, Earth Sciences, Biological Sciences, Computer Science and Engineering at the University of Bristol.

Satellites now return far more data about the Earth than any team could examine by hand. Our research builds the methods that turn those measurements into answers: detecting the ground deformation that precedes a volcanic eruption, mapping floods as they spread, tracking forest change and glacier retreat, and reading the sea surface through radar. We work across the whole chain, from the physics of the sensor to the models that interpret what it sees.

Our vision is Earth observation that is timely, trustworthy and useful beyond the laboratory. We develop methods that are honest about their uncertainty, that hold up in places they were never trained on, and that can run operationally alongside the scientists and agencies monitoring natural hazards and a changing climate. We collaborate widely across the world, and we train the next generation of researchers working at the meeting point of AI and the Earth Observation.
